TOOLBENCH

Quickly calculate percentages, proportions, percentage changes, and value adjustments.

1. Percentage of a Number
What is % of ? 37.5
Formula: (15 × 250) ÷ 100 = 37.5
2. Proportion as Percentage
is what % of ? 25%
Formula: (45 ÷ 180) × 100 = 25%
3. Percentage Change (Increase / Decrease)
From to is +25%
Formula: ((100 - 80) ÷ |80|) × 100 = +25% (Increase)
4. Add or Subtract Percentage
% = 132
Formula: 120 + (120 × 10%) = 132
